Feature Engineering & Preprocessing Highlights

• Cyclical Time Features:

Sin/Cos transformations for arrival month and day of week to accurately capture annual seasonal oscillations.

• Lead Time Non-Linearity:

Log-transformed lead time and segmented risk buckets to accommodate extreme reservations up to 500+ days.

• Target Leakage Prevention:

Exclusion of post-booking status (`reservation_status`, `reservation_status_date`) to ensure strictly pre-arrival operational inference.

• Data Source Harmonization:

Reconciled legacy baby/child fields, standardized meal plans, and preserved 31,994 legitimate identical booking patterns.
